Privacy on this site.

This page describes the Bleenky marketing website. The linked salon dashboard and booking pages run separately.

Browsing

This version does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, a contact-form database, or browser storage. Images, styles, and scripts are served with the website. Like other websites, the hosting service may process technical request information, such as an IP address and requested page, in server logs.

Contacting Bleenky

When a contact email is configured, the inquiry button opens your email application with a suggested subject and message. It does not send a message automatically. Information you choose to send is received through the configured email provider so Bleenky can discuss your inquiry. Do not include payment card details or sensitive customer records.

Linked demos and booking tools

Links to salon demos and the staff dashboard take you to a separately hosted service. Signing in or making a real booking there may involve additional information. The independent concept pages label their simulated booking flows. Review the information shown on the destination page before entering personal details.

Questions

Use the contact option on the homepage to ask about information you have shared with Bleenky. If you are viewing the pre-launch preview before a contact address is connected, inquiries are not yet open.

Launch review: confirm the actual host, contact provider, operator details, and data handling practices before treating this page as the final privacy notice. Update it if forms, analytics, or tracking are added.
