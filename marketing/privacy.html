A SIMPLE WEBSITE. A CLEAR EXPLANATION.

Privacy on this site.

This page describes the Bleenky marketing website. The linked salon dashboard and booking pages run separately.

Browsing

This version does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts, a contact-form database, or advertising-related browser storage. Images, styles, and scripts are served with the website. Like other websites, the hosting service may process technical request information, such as an IP address and requested page, in server logs.

Contacting Bleenky

The interest form sends your name, email address, salon name, optional website, selected interest, and message to Bleenky through our Hostinger web server and email service. We use these details to respond to your inquiry. Inquiry contents are not saved in a website database; they are delivered to our mailbox. The form uses a secure session cookie to protect submissions and short-lived request timestamps and hashed network addresses to limit spam. Records older than one hour are ignored and removed when subsequent submissions are processed. The email link is an alternative that opens your own email application. Do not include payment card details or sensitive customer records.

Linked demos and booking tools

The salon demos run on demos.bleenky.com; the staff dashboard runs on a separate service. Signing in or making a real booking there may involve additional information. The independent concept pages label their simulated booking flows. Review the information shown on the destination page before entering personal details.

Questions

Use the contact option on the homepage to ask about information you have shared with Bleenky. Email hello@bleenky.com with privacy questions.

Launch review: confirm the actual host, contact provider, operator details, and data handling practices before treating this page as the final privacy notice. Update it if forms, analytics, or tracking are added.
