GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Let’s clear
a few things up.

A website should make sense for your business. Here’s what to expect—and what isn’t included.

Read the service guide
How does the first month free work?

Your first month is 30 days free from the agreed website launch date. There is no setup fee. The website-only plan is then $29/month if you choose to continue. We confirm your choice before paid service begins; this website does not automatically enroll or charge you. Native booking is separate.

Can we cancel anytime?

Yes. Email hello@bleenky.com to cancel. During the free month, there is no monthly or setup fee to pay. Afterward, cancel before the next renewal to stop future charges; service continues through the paid period. Already-paid periods are not prorated, except where required by law.

We already have a website. Why change?

You may not need to. We’ll look at whether your current site makes your services, location, and next steps clear. If it already does what you need, there’s no reason to rebuild just for the sake of it.

We’re fully booked. Is this still useful?

A website can explain your services and answer common questions without asking you to take more appointments. You can keep booking by phone or link to your existing system. Native online booking is optional.

Can we keep Square or another booking system?

Yes. We can link your website’s booking button to your existing booking page. That is a link, not automatic calendar synchronization. Direct Square integration is not included in the website offer.

What if grooming times vary from pet to pet?

We’ll define services around the pet sizes and care needs you can schedule reliably, with cleanup buffers. Visits that need an assessment can remain phone-only. Shared wash-bay capacity is not part of the current groomer scheduling system.

Can customers double-book a groomer?

The separate native booking system checks for overlaps and reserves service time plus the cleanup buffer. It does not automatically know about appointments in another system, so phone and external bookings must be accounted for in the schedule.

Do you send confirmation emails and reminders?

For the separate booking service, not yet. Native booking provides an on-screen confirmation and a calendar download. Automated email and SMS delivery are not included. Customers should save their confirmation, and staff must contact them directly about changes.

Who needs an account?

The website-only offer requires no booking account. For the separate booking service, staff currently use invited ChatGPT accounts to sign in to Bleenky’s native dashboard. Customers can book without an account. We’ll check that this works for your team before you commit to native booking.

What do we own, and what happens if we cancel?

You retain your domain and supplied content. On request, we provide the agreed salon-specific website files for handoff, with no setup payment required under this offer. Moving to another host is separately scoped. Native booking software is separate and is not part of the website-file handoff.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No. We commit to the agreed website and functionality, not a promised number of clients, revenue, or search ranking. The first question is whether the service fits what your salon needs.
