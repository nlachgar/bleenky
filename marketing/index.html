GOOD QUESTIONS. STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

Let’s clear
a few things up.

A website should make sense for your business. Here’s what to expect—and what isn’t included.

Explore the service guide ↗
How does the 30-day free trial work?

Your first 30 days of monthly service are free, starting when the website launches. The New Year offer has a separate $0.99 one-time setup fee. After the trial, Website & Care is $99/month and Website & Booking is $199/month if you choose to continue. We confirm your choice before paid service begins; there is no automatic checkout or card charge on this website.

What if we decide not to continue?

Tell Bleenky before the trial ends if you want to stop. No monthly fee is due for the trial, and the $100 standard setup fee is not reinstated. The $0.99 setup fee is separate from the free monthly-service trial. See the service guide for setup cancellation and handoff details.

We already have a website. Why change?

You may not need to. We’ll look at whether your current site makes your services, location, and next steps clear. If it already does what you need, there’s no reason to rebuild just for the sake of it.

We’re fully booked. Is this still useful?

A website can explain your services and answer common questions without asking you to take more appointments. You can keep booking by phone or link to your existing system. Native online booking is optional.

Can we keep Square or another booking system?

Yes. We can link your website’s booking button to your existing booking page. That is a link, not automatic calendar synchronization. Direct Square integration is not built or included in these plans.

What if grooming times vary from pet to pet?

We’ll define services around the pet sizes and care needs you can schedule reliably, with cleanup buffers. Visits that need an assessment can remain phone-only. Shared wash-bay capacity is not part of the current groomer scheduling system.

Can customers double-book a groomer?

The native system checks for overlaps and reserves service time plus the cleanup buffer. It does not automatically know about appointments in another system, so phone and external bookings must be accounted for in the schedule.

Do you send confirmation emails and reminders?

Not yet. Native booking provides an on-screen confirmation and a calendar download. Automated email and SMS delivery are not included. Customers should save their confirmation, and staff must contact them directly about changes.

Who needs an account?

Staff currently use invited ChatGPT accounts to sign in to Bleenky’s native dashboard. Customers can book without an account. We’ll check that this works for your team before you commit to native booking.

What do we own, and what happens if we cancel?

You retain your domain and supplied content. After full setup payment, the agreed salon-specific website files are included in the handoff. The shared booking software remains Bleenky’s platform and is available while subscribed. Website files do not include a portable copy of the shared booking system. See the service guide for the proposed handoff process.

Will this guarantee more customers?

No. We commit to the agreed website and functionality, not a promised number of clients, revenue, or search ranking. The first question is whether the service fits what your salon needs.
