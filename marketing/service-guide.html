CLEAR EXPECTATIONS, FROM THE START

The service guide.

What goes into your website, what happens after launch, and what to expect if you decide to leave.

Proposed commercial terms for review. These details are not an accepted customer agreement. Your final written scope should confirm the selected plan, deliverables, fees, and service arrangements before work begins.

01. What the plans cover

Both plans include a 30-day free trial of the monthly service. After the trial, Website & Care is $99 per month and Website & Booking is $199 per month if you choose to continue. The standard one-time setup fee is $100 for either plan; the New Year promotional setup fee is $0.99. This is a reduced fee, not a waived fee. Prices are in USD. Both cover one salon and one website page with up to seven sections, responsive layouts, hosting, HTTPS, and maintenance of the agreed features.

Website & Care can link to your existing booking page. Website & Booking adds native scheduling for up to five bookable groomers, services and durations, cleanup buffers, availability rules, and instant appointment confirmation. Additional pages, locations, staff onboarding beyond the included scope, and integrations require a separate quote.

02. Your trial, setup, and payment

The $0.99 promotional setup fee is due before setup begins. Your 30-day free trial of monthly service starts on the agreed website launch date, not when you first inquire or while the site is being built. We provide the trial start and end dates in writing. The trial does not cover the setup fee or third-party charges.

If you choose to continue, the selected plan’s monthly fee begins after the trial: $99 for Website & Care or $199 for Website & Booking. Bleenky confirms your choice and payment arrangements before starting paid service. If you do not choose to continue, hosted trial service ends after the trial; no automatic paid enrollment is created by this website.

Delivery timing is agreed after reviewing the scope and receiving the required business details, photos, and feedback. There is no automated checkout, trial activation, card collection, or subscription billing on this marketing website.

Domain registration, external subscriptions, payment-provider fees, and other third-party charges are separate unless explicitly included in the quote.

03. Design revisions and changes

Two consolidated revision rounds are included before launch. A round is one collected set of feedback on the agreed design and content. New pages, a substantially different design direction, or new functionality are separately scoped.

After launch, Website & Care includes up to 30 minutes of content updates per month; Website & Booking includes up to 60 minutes. Examples include supplied-photo swaps, price changes, and edits to existing text. Unused time does not roll over. Extra work is quoted for approval before it begins. Corrections to defects in the agreed implementation do not use this content allowance.

04. Support

Email support is proposed for Monday–Friday, excluding holidays, with a target initial response within two business days. Response time is not a guaranteed resolution time. There is no 24/7 service or guaranteed uptime commitment included in these plans. Third-party outages may require resolution by the relevant provider.

Report the affected page, what you were doing, and any error message. Avoid sending payment card details or unnecessary customer information.

05. Your content, domain, and website

You retain your domain and the materials you supply. Your domain should be registered in an account you control. Once the setup fee is fully paid, the agreed salon-specific website files are included in the handoff.

Bleenky retains its reusable components and shared booking platform. Third-party assets remain subject to their own licenses. A copy of the marketing website does not include a portable copy of the hosted booking database, authentication system, or shared software.

06. Cancellation and handoff

To stop during the trial, contact Bleenky before your written trial end date. No monthly service fee is charged for the trial period. Continuing after the trial requires your confirmation; ending the trial does not reinstate the $100 standard setup fee.

The proposed monthly arrangement can be cancelled before the next renewal, with service continuing through the paid period and no subsequent monthly charges. Already-paid monthly periods are not prorated under this proposed offer. The New Year setup charge is $0.99 in total; there is no later catch-up charge for the $100 standard setup fee. If you cancel before setup work begins, the setup payment is refunded. Once the agreed setup work is completed, that fee covers the completed work and is separate from the free monthly-service trial. Any other agreed work must be separately quoted before it begins.

On request, the handoff includes the agreed website files after full setup payment and a salon-specific export of appointment and contact records. The export is a manual service, not a self-service dashboard feature. Agree on export timing and delivery with Bleenky before service ends. Setting up another host, replacing booking software, or importing data into another provider is separately scoped.

Export only your salon’s data, and arrange a new booking process before hosted booking access ends. The final agreement should confirm data retention and deletion arrangements; this website does not establish a retention period.

07. Booking boundaries

Native scheduling reserves one appointment at a time per groomer, including the configured cleanup buffer. Salon staff must keep working hours, service durations, and phone appointments accurate. Availability in outside systems is not synchronized automatically.

Staff sign in with invited ChatGPT accounts. Customers do not need an account. Confirmation appears on screen and can be downloaded as a calendar event. Email/SMS delivery, deposits, direct Square synchronization, and shared wash-station capacity are not included. Staff currently change a visit by cancelling it and booking a new time.

Salons must approve their real business information, services, and hours before enabling customer booking. The example salon websites are independent concepts and do not represent commissioned projects or endorsements.

08. What success means

Bleenky provides the agreed website and functionality. Customer numbers, revenue, search rankings, and reductions in administration are not guaranteed. Advertising, ongoing search marketing, professional photography, and legal-document preparation are outside the stated plans.
