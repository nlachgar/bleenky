CLEAR EXPECTATIONS, FROM THE START

The service guide.

What goes into your website, what happens after launch, and what to expect if you decide to leave.

The website offer is approved. Confirm your deliverables, trial dates, and service arrangements in writing before work begins. This guide does not itself enroll you in service.

01. The website offer

Your website-only plan includes your first month free and no setup fee, then costs $29 per month if you choose to continue. Prices are in USD. The offer covers one salon and one template-based page with up to seven sections, customized with supplied branding, services, photos, and contact details, plus hosting, HTTPS, and maintenance of the agreed features.

We confirm the website scope in writing before work begins. Domain registration and third-party subscriptions are separate.

The website can link to your existing booking system. Native scheduling, booking software, additional pages, extra locations, and custom integrations are not included; they require a separate agreed scope and price.

02. Your free month and payment

Your first month is 30 days from the agreed website launch date, not the first inquiry or the start of development. We provide the start and end dates in writing. There is no setup fee and no setup charge if you cancel.

After the free month, the website-only service costs $29 per month if you choose to continue. We confirm your choice and payment arrangements before paid service begins. If you do not continue, hosted service ends after the free period. This website does not automatically enroll you in a paid plan or collect payment details.

Delivery timing is agreed after reviewing the scope and receiving your business information, photos, and feedback. Third-party costs are not covered by the free month.

03. Design revisions and updates

Two consolidated revision rounds are included before launch. A round is one collected set of feedback on the agreed design and content. New pages, a different design direction, and new functionality require a separate quote.

The website plan includes one small content update per month, up to 15 minutes: for example, a supplied-photo swap or a change to existing prices or text. Unused time does not roll over. Extra work is quoted for approval before it begins. Corrections to defects in the agreed implementation do not use this allowance.

04. Support

Contact hello@bleenky.com for email support Monday–Friday, excluding holidays. The target initial response is within two business days; this is not a guaranteed resolution time. There is no 24/7 service or guaranteed uptime commitment. Third-party outages may require resolution by the relevant provider.

Include the affected page and a description of the issue. Avoid sending payment card details or unnecessary customer information.

05. Your content, domain, and website

You retain your domain and supplied materials. Keep your domain registered in an account you control. On request, we provide the agreed salon-specific website files for handoff; no setup payment is required under this offer.

Bleenky retains its reusable components and shared booking platform. Third-party assets remain subject to their licenses. Website files do not include the separately hosted booking database, authentication system, or shared booking software.

06. Cancel anytime

Email hello@bleenky.com to cancel at any time. During the free month, there is no monthly or setup fee to pay. Continuing afterward requires your confirmation.

After paid service begins, cancel before the next renewal to stop future charges. Service continues through the paid period. Already-paid periods are not prorated, except where required by law. There is no long-term contract, cancellation penalty, or later setup charge.

Request your website files and agree handoff timing before hosting ends. Setting up another host or importing files into another provider is separately scoped. Data export, retention, and cancellation arrangements for any separate booking service must be agreed before that service begins.

07. Booking is separate

The $29/month offer does not include native booking. Your website can link to an existing booking page without synchronizing its calendar. Native booking has a separate scope and price agreed before work begins.

The existing native platform supports separate groomer schedules, service durations, cleanup buffers, instant confirmation, and calendar downloads. Staff use invited ChatGPT accounts; customers can book without an account. Email/SMS reminders, deposits, Square synchronization, and shared wash-station capacity are not included.

We review suitability, real services and hours, staff access, and data handling before enabling production booking. Demo bookings remain simulated; the example salons are independent concepts, not endorsements.

08. What success means

Bleenky provides the agreed website and functionality. Customer numbers, revenue, and search rankings are not guaranteed. Advertising, ongoing search marketing, professional photography, and legal-document preparation are outside this offer.
